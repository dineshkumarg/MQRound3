What changed in this version

  • Real vacancies added: the KNRUHS CAT-B & C seat matrix after Phase 3 (16.10.2026) gives the actual number of LOCAL (OU) MBBS seats still open at each college. Only the LOCAL column is used; UR (unreserved, open to non-locals) and NRI seats are ignored.
  • How P3 is now estimated: the open local seats are filled by the next candidates after the Phase 2 closing S.No. Each college's own draw density (seats per S.No, from real Phase 1+2) converts "seats" into "S.No". Example: 5 vacancies at density 0.24 reach about 21 S.No past the P2 closing.
  • Round 3 eligibility applied: 205 local candidates were removed from the competing pool: those named in the CQ Phase 3 (mop-up) list (rule 3) and the local MBBS names on the official MQ Phase 2 Not Reported / Discontinued / Free Exit list (rule 1). Everyone below them moves up. The pool is now 3289 (was 3,494). Closing S.No values, the S.No-to-score map and each college's draw density were all rebuilt on the smaller pool. College cutoff scores did not change.
  • Replaced: the earlier CQ-pattern slip estimate. It is still shown as a tooltip on the P3 cell for comparison.
How the projection works

Real Phase 1 and Phase 2 (never projected)

  • Each college's Phase 1 and Phase 2 closing score / S.No is the worst score among real, genuine local general candidates (CTB-LOC-OPEN-GEN-P1 and -P2 tags only; NRI, minority, women-only and reservation seats are excluded).
  • Your S.No is converted to your live local standing (out of 3289), the pool of local candidates still eligible for Round 3 web options: Phase 1 Not Reported candidates who never resurfaced everyone named in the CQ Phase 3 (mop-up) list, and Phase 2 not-reported or discontinued local MBBS candidates are removed.

Phase 3 estimate (the only estimated number)

  • Real Round 3 local vacancies come from the official seat matrix (CAT-B LOCAL column). P3 closing S.No = P2 closing S.No + vacancies ÷ college density, capped at the end of the live pool, then mapped to a score.
  • Density is the college's real Phase 1+2 draw: how many of its seats fell per S.No across the P1→P2 range. Colleges with 0 vacancies keep P3 = P2.
  • Phase 3 can never be tighter than the real Phase 2 closing.

Feasibility rating

  • Very Safe — your live standing clears the real P1 closing. Safe — clears the real P2 closing. Borderline — only the projected P3 would take you. Very Risky — below projected P3.

Things to keep in mind

  • Not applied (no data): rule 2, candidates who never exercised Phase 1 web options (the not-allotted lists do not separate them from candidates who did); rule 5, MCC admissions. These would shrink the pool further and help you slightly.
  • Vacancies are real but who takes them is not. Candidates above the P2 closing who hold a lower college can also upgrade into a vacant seat, and the wish-order of the remaining candidates is unknown, so the estimate can be optimistic for a popular college with few seats. Treat Borderline as a real chance, not a promise.
  • Some vacancies likely come from candidates who took a CQ Round 3 seat and gave up their MQ seat; they are already inside the real vacancy numbers.
  • Excludes: Deccan, Shadan, Ayaan (minority-only), Dr VRK Women's, Raja Rajeshwari (women-only) and all dental colleges. Non-local candidates are excluded throughout.
